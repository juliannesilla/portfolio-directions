05 — Rules

Do & Don't

Do

  • Lead with bold uppercase statements + one serif-italic accent.
  • Use #CDAD7D gold for emphasis, not as a fill.
  • Lots of charcoal negative space; let stats + photography breathe.
  • Keep motion buttery — performance over spectacle.
  • Use only real photos / real case-study graphics.

Don't

  • Don't use the pink/purple personal-brand palette here.
  • Don't use text-baked cover images as headshots.
  • Don't run heavy full-screen animated filters (lag).
  • Don't soften the voice — it's confident, not corporate-bland.
  • Don't invent metrics or case studies.

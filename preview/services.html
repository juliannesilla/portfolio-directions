The Process

How I work

A proven five-phase framework — from first brief to repeatable playbook.

01

GTM Architecture

+

We start by getting ruthlessly clear on who we're targeting, what problem we're solving, and how the offer lands against the competitive landscape. ICP definition, positioning, offer structure, and funnel map — locked before a single asset is written.

02

Pre-Launch Warmup

+

Audience priming across email and organic channels — warming cold subscribers, building anticipation, and setting up the nurture sequences that qualify leads before the cart opens. No launch succeeds without a warm audience.

03

Multi-Channel Launch

+

Coordinated execution across email, paid, and organic — timed to hit simultaneously and reinforce each touchpoint. Checkout optimization, urgency copy, and cart abandonment sequences fire in real time to maximize conversion at peak intent.

04

Post-Launch Nurture

+

The launch ends — the revenue engine doesn't. Lifecycle sequences, retention flows, win-back campaigns, and lead recovery automation keep your audience engaged and your pipeline full between launches.

05

Optimization & Playbooks

+

Post-mortem analysis against every KPI, UTM attribution audit, and a documented SOP library — so your next launch runs faster, tighter, and with predictable results. Strategy that compounds every cycle.

Every engagement ends with a repeatable playbook — not just a one-time result.
